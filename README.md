## Research interests

My background is in business intelligence, and I want to specialize in quantitative risk. I'm interested in how portfolio risk estimates change when volatility rises or assets that usually move independently start falling together.

- **Market risk.** How do historical simulation and volatility-based models compare when estimating a portfolio's one-day Value at Risk (VaR) and Expected Shortfall?
- **Stress testing.** How sensitive are estimated losses to assumptions about volatility and correlations during a market sell-off?
- **Model validation.** When a VaR model forecasts a 1% chance of exceeding its loss threshold, do breaches occur roughly once in 100 trading days? Do they cluster during turbulent periods?
